# Known limitations before controlled development testing

Reviewed 5 November 2026 (Asia/Kolkata). These are observed code/configuration facts or explicitly unverified behavior. No fix, permission change, deployment, or publication is included. See [release readiness](RELEASE_READINESS.md) for the proposed causal-report design and [development test plan](DEVELOPMENT_TEST_PLAN.md) for acceptance cases and monitoring requirements.

## Readiness holds

Before a new controlled development deployment, review the ignored manifest's action inputs/scopes/app identity, approve the test-data boundary, verify the source rollback checkpoint, and validate the test monitoring path. Keep all production deployments/configuration and publication outside that approval.

Before changing causal-report behavior, approve the versioned evidence/citation/reviewer contract and its positive/negative tests. A supported cause must not be accepted solely because a model is confident, a critic agrees, or references exist. Preserve Unknown whenever the causal chain, scope, material coverage, contradictions, or trusted acceptance is insufficient.
