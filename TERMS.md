It is an investigation aid, not an autonomous authority for production, security, legal, compliance, employment, financial, or customer-impacting decisions.

Users remain responsible for validating investigation results and deciding whether to take any operational action. A generated hypothesis or suggested root cause should not be treated as established fact unless supported by sufficient evidence.

The Community Edition Jira action is designed to be read-only. Users should independently review evidence before deploying code, changing infrastructure, modifying customer data, communicating a root cause, or taking other consequential action.
